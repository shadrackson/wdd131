Comrades Banking Solutions

Comrades Banking Solutions represents a modern, inclusive approach to digital banking built on trust, collaboration, and accessibility. The name “Comrades” conveys unity and shared progress, reflecting the platform’s commitment to serving students, professionals, and entrepreneurs alike. It emphasizes a sense of community and mutual support among users while maintaining a professional tone suitable for financial services. This identity positions the brand as a reliable partner for individuals seeking secure, convenient, and user‑friendly remote banking solutions.

Site Logo

Site Purpose

The website will allow customers to:

  • Select account types (savings, current, student, business)
  • Set up accounts remotely (filling digital forms, uploading documents, Identity Verification)
  • Manage accounts (balance checks, transaction history, funds transfer, card requests)
  • Get customer support (live chat, FAQs, hotline support)

Scenarios

Scenario 1: Student Account Setup

"I am a student and I want to open up a student account remotely. What documents do I need to upload, and how long will it take before I can start using my account?"

Scenario 2: Small Business Owner

"I run a small online business and I need a current account to manage payments. Can I open a business account here, and will I be able to transfer funds and request a debit card online?"

Color Schema

Carefully selected color schema for the site which is in harmony with the brand identity and the users are as follows:

Color Hex Code Usage
Primary Blue #1E3A8A Headings, navigation bar, key buttons
Accent Orange #F97316 Call-to-action highlights, hover effects, notices
Neutral Light Gray #F3F4F6 Backgrounds, form sections, FAQ panels
Dark Gray Text #111827 Paragraphs, transaction details, FAQs

Typography

Font Usage Objective
Montserrat Headings, navigation menus, call-to-action buttons Modern and bold, gives a professional yet approachable look. Great for emphasis and hierarchy
Roboto Body text, paragraphs, FAQs, transaction details Highly readable on screens, neutral tone, ensures clarity for long-form content.
Merriweather Special sections (quotes, testimonials, highlighted info boxes) Adds contrast and sophistication. Using a serif font sparingly creates emphasis and variety.
