Our Journey

Our Beginning

Comrades Banking Solutions has roots tracing back to the mid-19th century. In 1892, Professor Hannington Young and Blitz S. Onyango started a small initiative to help folks with limited financial access achieve their goals, laying the foundation for modern banking.

Growth & Innovation

Over the years, we have embraced digital transformation, offering modern banking solutions including online banking, mobile access, and fast financial services. Our focus remains on innovation, trust, and customer satisfaction.
